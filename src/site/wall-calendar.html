Space Calendar

Make a wall calendar

A year of sky for your location, to print or laser engrave.

Options

The page is 16 × 12 in. It stays sharp at any size that keeps the 4:3 shape.

The engraving file has a red outline of the page. Set it to Ignore before you engrave.

"Copy link" makes a link that opens this calendar, so it includes the location.

Your browser draws the calendar. Your location is not sent to this site.
